HOW THE CENTER WORKS

EVENT → CARD → CHAMPIONSHIP

Public sports events are the entry point. UFC events can expose stored bout and fighter intelligence. WWE and AEW events resolve into the dedicated wrestling-event record before loading synchronized matches. Championship and reign information is kept in the wrestling data layer rather than mixed into team-game scoring.
